Questions, answered.

Is this really free?

Yes. Everything is free, and it stays that way. No card required.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Sabal runs in your browser, on your phone or computer.

How close is it to the real exam?

Practice questions mirror the FCLE’s format and the four tested domains. It’s the closest thing to sitting the exam without sitting it.

What if I’m completely out of practice?

That’s exactly who Sabal is for. Most students just need a refresher and the right practice — the Diagnostic shows where to start, and every answer explains the why.

I’ve already failed this exam. Why would this time be different?

Failing it more than once is rarely about ability — it’s usually about the practice. Drilling questions trains you to recognize the question, but the FCLE is written so the wrong answers sound right, and that only yields to understanding the why. So every Sabal answer explains why the choice that felt right was wrong, the Diagnostic shows which few topics are costing you the most points, and the readiness score tells you when you’re actually ready instead of hoping. We won’t promise you a score — no prep honestly can.

How is this different from the free Quizlet sets and YouTube videos?

Free question sets do one job well — they tell you whether you got it right. What they can’t tell you is why the answer you picked felt reasonable, and on this exam that’s the whole difficulty: the wrong choices are written to sound plausible. Sabal explains the reasoning behind the correct answer and the tempting ones, then tracks which topics keep showing up in your misses, so you’re working on a pattern instead of a hunch. And since nothing in a free deck keeps score over time, it can’t tell you when you’re ready. That’s the part we built.

How long should I study before I sit the exam again?

Long enough that the topics you keep missing stop being the topics you miss. Retakes are gated at 24 hours, but nothing about this exam rewards rushing back — what changes your result is whether the pattern of your missed topics changes. So watch your topic results: when the same few subjects stop showing up in your misses, that’s your signal to go sit it. Better than picking a date and hoping.

Why is it called Sabal?

Sabal is the sabal palmetto — Florida’s state tree. It grows where other trees won’t, and it bends in a storm instead of breaking. It’s also the palm Florida law names in the state’s Great Seal, which felt like the right standard for a Florida graduation requirement.
